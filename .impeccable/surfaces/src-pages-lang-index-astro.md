# Surface brief: La Rusticana site (home + inner pages)

Mode: Persuade. Visitor decides "eat here tonight" and reserves (call / WhatsApp / form).
Audience: Los Belones / La Manga Club residents, holidaymakers, golfers, families; es/en/de/fr(/nl).
Proof on hand: since 1986, family run, Atamaría, terraces, play areas, own parking, ~4.6 Google. No first-party photos yet: illustrative art is labelled synthetic; owner supplies photos.
Memorable moment: hand-plaited esparto (pleita) braid bands frame every section; menu categories are tied paper tags.
Unresolved: real hero photo, opening hours truth, legal entity, nl inclusion, social profile confirmation.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Pleita. The site is one plaited-esparto object: braid bands (SVG plait pattern) divide sections and menu categories are twine-tied tags. Refuses the dark, gold, food-photo-wall restaurant template and card grids.
OWN-WORLD: straw ground #F4EEDD, esparto tan plait #D9C79A, drenched olive-green fields #2F3A1E/#46512A, stone-brown secondary text, terracotta #B0512B only on the reserve action and small marks. Brygada 1918 for headings, Hanken Grotesk for text. Flat, matte, no shadows or gradients, hard corners, tags with a punched hole and a twine line, hairline stitch borders.
STORY: The visitor sees a family restaurant since 1986 beside La Manga Club, trusts it through real history and facts, and taps Reservar, Llamar or Cómo llegar.
FIRST VIEWPORT: 100svh olive field. Plait band under the header. Three layered olive sierra silhouettes fill the lower half with an olive tree and a low sun. LA RUSTICANA set huge in serif lower-left, tagline beneath, terracotta "Reservar mesa" plus outlined "Ver carta" directly below, and a one-line place stamp "Atamaría · La Manga Club · Cartagena". Photo slot replaces the illustration when the owner supplies a picture.
FORM: candidate 6 of 7 (1 hillside terrace at dusk, 2 venta wall board, 3 Gasthaus Speisekarte, 4 golf yardage book, 5 Atamaría herbarium, 6 esparto pleita, 7 Mar Menor salt chart); seed key 200375ea. Raises: scale-jump discipline from the Greiman challenger (huge name against footnote-size labels), three-dyes-only discipline from the depot blind (green, straw, terracotta dot), whole-module grid from the azulejo hall (plait module drives spacing).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
